Which One Do You Want?

If you want a traditional card-based rummy experience with both quick points-rummy hands and longer deals-rummy sessions, that's IND Rummy. If you want a slots/reel-spin experience with symbol matching and multiplier rounds instead of cards at all, that's IND Slots. Mechanically the three aren't interchangeable despite the matching name prefix and similar-looking bonus figures — two are card games and one is a slots game.
