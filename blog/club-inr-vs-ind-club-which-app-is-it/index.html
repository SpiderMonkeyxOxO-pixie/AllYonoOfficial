By All Yono Official Editorial Team · Identity · Published 20 November 2026 · How we check facts

Club INR and IND Club are two separate apps listed on this site — not the same app with a typo in the name. Look closely and the two names use the exact same two words, just in reversed order, with one word swapped by a single letter: "Club INR" opens with Club and closes with the abbreviation INR; "IND Club" opens with the abbreviation IND and closes with Club. That mirrored structure is exactly why a forwarded referral link or a quick search for one can easily be mistaken for the other. Why These Names Get Confused

Club INR and IND Club use the same two building blocks — the word "Club" and a short abbreviation — arranged in reversed order: Club INR puts "Club" first and the abbreviation second, while IND Club puts the abbreviation first and "Club" second. The abbreviations themselves, INR and IND, differ by only a single letter. That's a different confusion pattern from the shared-prefix naming seen in IND Rummy, IND Club and IND Slots, or the shared-suffix naming seen in Slots Winner and Spin Winner — here it's the word order that flips along with a one-letter swap, which is arguably easier to misread at a glance. Each app's own landing page on this site also follows the same visual template — identical ~85 MB size, identical 18+ age rating, and an identical disclosure box — so the pages themselves can look interchangeable even though they're two separate listings with their own domain and referral link.
