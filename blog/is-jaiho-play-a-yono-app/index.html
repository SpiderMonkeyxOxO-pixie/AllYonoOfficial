Is Jaiho Play a Yono App? What We Found

By All Yono Official Editorial Team · App Comparisons

Published: October 24, 2026  |  Last reviewed: October 24, 2026

Launched October 15, 2026

Jaiho Play shares the green-diamond, gold-lettering look used across several listings in this directory, and it names Yonotech Pvt. Ltd. as its operator — which is enough to make people assume it's officially part of the "Yono" family. Here's what's actually confirmed and what isn't.

What Jaiho Play Discloses

Jaiho Play launched 15 October 2026 (version 1.0.0) with rummy and Teen Patti. It discloses a minimum deposit of about ₹50–₹100, a minimum withdrawal of ₹100, a welcome bonus range of ₹50–₹500 (conditions not recorded), and UPI, Paytm and BHIM as payment methods. It names Yonotech Pvt. Ltd. as its operator — a claim we have not independently verified against any other Yono-branded listing's own operator records.

Why a Named Operator Doesn't Settle It

A listed operator name is a disclosed fact, not proof of a shared company across every "Yono"-adjacent app. Several apps in this directory use similar branding, similar operator-style naming, or both, without one confirmed to be run by the same company as another. Three things would actually show a real connection: matching operator details across both apps' own terms, official sources that reference each other, or a direct statement from an operator confirming the relationship. None of those exist here yet.

How to Avoid Assuming a Connection That Isn't Confirmed

  • Compare the exact app name on your install screen against the page that gave you the file.
  • Check the operator name Jaiho Play discloses (Yonotech Pvt. Ltd.) rather than assuming it matches another app's listed operator.
  • Don't assume a promo code or bonus figure from one app applies to another — see our promo code safety guide.
  • Treat the ₹50–₹500 bonus range as disclosed, not verified, since its conditions aren't recorded.
