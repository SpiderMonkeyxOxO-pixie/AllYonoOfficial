Why a Shared Name Proves Little

A shared first word does not prove a shared operator, game list, payout system or terms. Brand names are cheap to reuse, and copycat naming is common. Three signals would actually show a link: the same named operator in the terms of both apps, matching official sources that reference each other, or a statement from the operator saying they are related. Without one of these, treat the apps as unrelated. Our guides on Jaiho 777 and Jaiho Rummy show how we document other apps in the family without assuming a connection.
