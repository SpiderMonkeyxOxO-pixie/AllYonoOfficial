ABC Rummy is a direct APK, not a Play Store app — that distribution model is exactly why repackaged copies circulate through group chats and comment sections. Referral Codes

Enter a code once at sign-up, and both you and whoever shared it can pick up a bonus — a short string of characters, nothing else. It never requires a password, OTP, or bank login to work, and anyone suggesting otherwise isn't offering a real referral bonus. See our referral code safety guide.

Logging In Safely

There's no web portal for ABC Rummy — account access is entirely in-app. A message asking you to "verify" through an external link isn't legitimate. Our login access notes cover this.

Withdrawals

₹100 minimum, paid via UPI or bank transfer from the in-app wallet. Check the transaction history inside the app first if a payout is delayed — not a message offering to release it. More in our withdrawal and payment safety guide.
