What the ₹180 Actually Covers

₹180 was the figure displayed on the JaiHo 777 VIP page at the time this guide was last updated — not a locked-in promise. Apps distributed outside the Play Store can and do adjust bonus terms without much notice, so whatever number greets you inside the app at sign-up is the one to go by, even where it doesn't line up with what's written here. Separately, a bonus or referral code only ever functions as a code: entering it does not, and should not, require handing over your password, an OTP, or your banking details alongside it. Anyone who asks for those in the same breath as a bonus offer is running a scam, not processing a reward.
